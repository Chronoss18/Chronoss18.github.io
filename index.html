Privacy Policy for NotYourNuggets, MergeTD, Baud Runner, and other games developed by MidnightDev

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile applications NotYourNuggets, MergeTD, and Baud Runner, and to other games developed by MidnightDev (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), created as free-to-play indie games.

Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed with user privacy in mind.

  • Personal Data: MidnightDev does not ask you to create an account. These games do not collect your postal address, your email address, or a precise GPS location.
  • NotYourNuggets, MergeTD, and other games: The Application may use Google Play Games for leaderboards and achievements. Google may process game data or device identifiers under Google's privacy policy.

Baud Runner

  • Advertising: Baud Runner shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you choose Reconnect after a failed run or PATCH during a chase. AdMob may receive the device's advertising ID and IP address to show and measure that ad.
  • Play Games: If you sign in, Google Play Games receives your player id, your Play Games name, your campaign-star score, and a cloud save named baud_runner_save. The museum achievements stay on the device.
  • Analytics: Analytics stay off until you turn on Share analytics in Settings. If you turn it on, level results and reward choices are sent to Google Firebase. You can turn the switch off again at any time.
  • Purchases: Baud Runner has no purchases.

Third Party Services

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Games
  • Google AdMob (Baud Runner)
  • Google Firebase (Baud Runner, only if you turn on Share analytics)

Children’s Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that necessary actions can be taken.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
